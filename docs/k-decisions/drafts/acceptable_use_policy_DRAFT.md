---

## 1. You must not

1. **Submit unreviewed Output to a regulator.** Do not use, file or submit any AI-generated Output as a regulatory filing, application document or regulatory correspondence unless it has first been reviewed and approved by a suitably qualified professional (ToS §5.3). This is the rule the rest of the product assumes.
2. **Scrape or bulk-extract the corpus.** Do not scrape, crawl, spider, or systematically download the regulatory corpus, templates, citations or enrichment data, whether manually or by automation, beyond the ordinary use of the Service's own features.
3. **Resell or redistribute corpus content or templates.** Do not resell, sublicense, republish or make available the corpus, the template library, or any substantial part of either, to any third party. (You **may** use and share the documents you generate for your or your client's own regulatory purposes — ToS §10.2.)
4. **Share credentials.** Accounts are per-named-user. Do not share logins or pool a single account across multiple people or firms. [TBD: seat/firm licensing language to follow the K6 pricing decision.]
5. **Build a competing dataset or model.** Do not use the Service or Output to train, fine-tune or evaluate a competing model or to assemble a competing regulatory database.
6. **Probe, overload or circumvent.** No penetration testing without written consent, no rate-limit circumvention, no automated query volumes intended to extract rather than use, no attempts to access other customers' data.
7. **Unlawful or deceptive use.** No use of the Service in connection with unlawful activity, and no representation that Output is human-authored professional advice or that Qanun endorses or has reviewed your filings.
